## Section: Why take it
- **Eyebrow:** Why take it
- **Heading:** Robotics the way industry does it

### Card: Real robots, every week
You won't spend the semester in simulation. You'll drive, map, and navigate with TurtleBot3s and Clearpath Jackals carrying LiDAR and IMUs.

### Card: The industry stack
ROS 2, Nav2, SLAM, Gazebo, RViz2, Python, and Git: the tools that show up in robotics and AV job postings.

### Card: The math behind autonomy
Kalman filters, sensor noise models, occupancy grids, and path planning. You'll understand *why* the robot does what it does, not just how to launch it.

### Card: A portfolio, not just a grade
Every project lives in a public GitHub repo with maps, videos, and interactive 3D results you can show employers.

## Section: Roadmap
- **Eyebrow:** The arc of the semester
- **Heading:** From sensors to self-driving

### Step 01: Where am I?
Dead reckoning, odometry, and Kalman filtering: estimating a robot's state from noisy sensors.

### Step 02: What's around me?
LiDAR sensor models, calibration, and building 2D and 3D maps of real spaces on campus.

### Step 03: How do I get there?
ROS 2 Nav2: SLAM, costmaps, keepout zones, global and local planners, and behavior trees for recovery.

### Step 04: Do it on your own, and do it well
Bring it all together in the Jackal Patrol Challenge.

## Section: Capstone
- **Eyebrow:** Capstone
- **Heading:** The Jackal Patrol Challenge
- **Paragraph:** Teams program a Clearpath Jackal to carry out an autonomous patrol mission with no joystick and no hand-holding. Robots are scored on how well they perform, so localization, planning, and recovery behavior all count.
- **Fine print:** Course rules and scoring will be announced in class.
- **Highlights:**
  - **Autonomous**: No teleoperation during scored runs
  - **Real hardware**: Jackal UGVs with LiDAR + IMU
  - **Scored**: Performance counts. Teams compete.
