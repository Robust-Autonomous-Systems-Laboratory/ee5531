EE5531 Introduction to Robotics

Take a robot from raw sensor data to driving itself. You'll localize, map, and navigate real robots with ROS 2 and Nav2, the same stack used on autonomous vehicles and robots in industry.

Built by students: 3D LiDAR map of Husky Plaza, about 2 million points

Real robots, every week

Clearpath Jackals and TurtleBot3s with LiDAR and IMUs. Not just simulation.

The industry toolchain

ROS 2, Nav2, SLAM, Gazebo, RViz2, Python, and Git: the skills in robotics and AV job postings.

The math behind autonomy

Kalman filters, sensor models, mapping, path planning, and behavior trees.

A portfolio you can show

Public GitHub repos with maps, videos, and interactive 3D results.

Jackal Patrol Challenge Your capstone: program a Jackal to patrol autonomously, then compete. Performance is scored.
